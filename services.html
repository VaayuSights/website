Our services

What we do

Data & intelligence

The core of the practice. These four run on imagery from any source — if you already fly, or have surveys sitting on a drive, send them over and we will take it from there. No capture of ours required.

AI Imagery Analysis

Computer-vision models that surface anomalies, hotspots and defects across thousands of frames in minutes, reviewed by our engineers.

Digital Twins

Photorealistic 3D reconstructions and point clouds — a living, measurable model of the asset you can inspect from your desk.

Analytics & Reporting

Trends across repeat surveys: how defects, volumes and site progress move over time, in dashboards and decision-ready reports.

AI/ML Data Annotation

Expert-labelled training data for detection, classification and segmentation — for your models or ours.

Alongside it

Aerial operations

When you need the capture as well, we scope the mission and brief a vetted partner operator to our spec, then process what comes back. Cleaning is different — those crews are our own.

Visual Inspection

High-resolution RGB capture of solar farms, turbines, towers, pipelines and roofs — at height, with nothing shut down and nobody on ropes.

Thermal Inspection

Radiometric thermal imaging that pinpoints faulty modules, overheating joints and insulation loss before they turn into failures.

Mapping & Surveying

Orthomosaics, contours and volumetrics to survey grade, flown with RTK and ground control for planning and progress tracking.

Aerial Cleaning

Drone cleaning for solar arrays, façades and glazing, roofs, silos and industrial structures — anywhere access is the hard part. Waterless or spray, with our own crews.

How it works

From data to decision

A repeatable four-step pipeline that turns raw aerial data into decisions you can defend.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Imagery arrives from you, your operator, or a partner flight we scope and brief to our capture spec.

  2. 02

    Process

    Photogrammetry & point-cloud engines build orthomosaics, 3D models and digital twins.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    AI/ML models detect, classify and geolocate defects — validated by domain experts.

  4. 04

    Report

    Actionable, prioritized reports and dashboards delivered straight to your teams.
