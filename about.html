About VaayuSights

Faster, safer, data-driven

We read aerial data for a living. Imagery comes in — from your team, your operator, or a capture we arrange — and ranked findings go out, so organizations improve safety, efficiency and asset performance.

Mission and vision

Our Mission

To make critical infrastructure safer and more productive by turning aerial data into decisions — faster, safer and smarter than manual inspection ever could.

Our Vision

A world where every high-value asset is watched from the sky and understood by AI — so failures are caught before they happen, and no one has to climb into harm's way to find them.

How it works

From flight to decision

A repeatable four-step pipeline that turns raw aerial data into decisions you can defend.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Imagery arrives from you, your operator, or a partner flight we scope and brief to our capture spec.

  2. 02

    Process

    Photogrammetry & point-cloud engines build orthomosaics, 3D models and digital twins.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    AI/ML models detect, classify and geolocate defects — validated by domain experts.

  4. 04

    Report

    Actionable, prioritized reports and dashboards delivered straight to your teams.
