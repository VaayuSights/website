What a VaayuSights program looks like

Typical engagements we are set up to run, and exactly what lands in your hands at the end of each one. We are a new company and publish client case studies only once we have delivered them — so nothing below is dressed up as work we have already done.

Typical engagements

Renewable Energy

Solar farm thermal audit

You send the thermal and RGB survey — flown by your own team, your operator, or a partner we brief — and we return AI hotspot detection and a module-level defect map. No capture of ours required, and the plant never stops.

You receive

  • Module-level defect map
  • Thermal + RGB orthomosaic
  • Severity-ranked repair list
  • Findings report with GPS references
Renewable Energy

Wind turbine blade inspection

Blade imagery classified for cracks, erosion and lightning damage across a wind farm. Capture arranged through a partner operator where you do not have one — nobody on ropes either way.

You receive

  • Blade-by-blade imagery
  • Classified defects with severity
  • Repair priority list
Infrastructure

Bridge structural survey

Crack mapping and a survey-grade 3D model built from aerial capture, so traffic keeps moving — no lane closures, and the imagery can come from whoever already flies the structure.

You receive

  • Crack and defect map
  • Survey-grade 3D model
  • Element condition notes
Commercial

Aerial cleaning programme

Scheduled drone cleaning of solar arrays, high-rise glazing, roofs and industrial structures, run by our own crews — no scaffolding, no rope teams, no access permits for closed floors.

You receive

  • Before / after capture
  • Coverage map per pass
  • Recommended cleaning interval
Industrial

Stockpile volumetric survey

Automated volumetrics over a materials yard, turning a day of manual measurement into one pass of imagery — processed from whatever survey you can get us.

You receive

  • Volume figure per stockpile
  • 3D surface model
  • Inventory reconciliation report
