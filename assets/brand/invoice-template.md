# Invoice template

Two versions, because the right one depends on whether the business is
registered for GST.

> **Not tax advice.** Confirm the specifics — SAC code, registration
> threshold, place-of-supply treatment — with a chartered accountant before
> the first invoice goes out. What follows is structure, not a ruling.

---

## Which version

| | |
|---|---|
| **A — Bill of Supply** | **Use this now.** No GST registration, so no GST may be charged |
| **B — Tax Invoice** | Once registered: GSTIN, SAC, and CGST+SGST or IGST |

**Do not charge GST without a GSTIN.** Collecting tax you are not registered
to collect is an offence, not a paperwork slip. If a client asks for a GST
invoice and you are not registered, say so plainly — it is common for small
suppliers and most procurement teams can handle it, though some cannot claim
input credit and may push you to register.

Registration becomes mandatory above the services turnover threshold, and
worth doing voluntarily before that if corporate clients need input credit.
Ask an accountant where that line sits for you.

## B — Tax Invoice (after registration)

Everything above, plus these, which a tax invoice must carry:

```
GSTIN              [ your GSTIN ]
Place of supply    [ state and code — decides IGST vs CGST+SGST ]
SAC code           [ confirm with your accountant ]

Taxable value                                       INR [ amount ]
CGST  [ % ]                                         INR [ amount ]   } intra-state
SGST  [ % ]                                         INR [ amount ]   }
IGST  [ % ]                                         INR [ amount ]     inter-state
TOTAL                                               INR [ amount ]
```

Intra-state supply splits into CGST and SGST; inter-state is IGST. Place of
supply is what decides it, and for services to a registered business it is
normally the client's location.

---

## Numbering

Keep it sequential, unique and unbroken within the financial year.

```
VS/2026-27/001
VS/2026-27/002
```

Never reuse or skip a number. If an invoice is cancelled, keep the number
and mark it cancelled rather than deleting it — gaps are the first thing an
auditor asks about.

---

## Practical

**Send the PDF, not an editable file.** An invoice that can be altered after
sending is a dispute waiting to happen.

**Invoice promptly.** On delivery, or on the milestone the proposal names.
Late invoicing trains clients to pay late.

**State the due date, not just the terms.** "Due 23 November" gets paid
sooner than "Net 15".

**Keep every copy.** Seven years is the usual retention expectation in India;
confirm with your accountant.

**Match the proposal.** Line items should echo the scope the client agreed.
An invoice that describes the work differently invites questions.
