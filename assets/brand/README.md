# VaayuSights — Brand Pack

Everything here is built from the VaayuSights logo. Replace anything in
`[ brackets ]` before use.

## What the company is

An aerial-data analysis practice. Drone imagery comes in from any source and
ranked findings go out. Capture is run by vetted partner operators we scope and
brief. Aerial Cleaning is ours outright, crews included.

The cleaning service is called **Aerial Cleaning**, never Panel Cleaning — it
covers solar arrays, facades and glazing, roofs, silos and industrial
structures, anywhere access is the hard part. Say "drone cleaning" in body copy,
where people search for it, but not as the name: it reads as cleaning the drones.

Anything written for the brand should lead with the analysis and keep that split
straight. Claiming a fleet reads stronger for one sentence and then falls apart
on the follow-up question.

## ⚠️ The one rule that matters

**The colour logo only works on dark backgrounds.** Its silver "VAAYU" and the
tagline rule are near-white, so on a white page they wash out and the lockup
falls apart. This is not a preference — it is a legibility failure.

| Background | Use |
|---|---|
| Dark (your site, decks, social) | `logo/vaayusights-full.png` — the colour logo |
| White or light | `logo/vaayusights-full-black.png` — the one-colour version |
| Dark, one-colour printing | `logo/vaayusights-full-white.png` |

If you want the colour logo on a light page, put it inside a dark panel first.

## The contact address

The published address is **pranaam@vaayusights.in** — a greeting, not a generic
inbox, and it suits an India-first business better than hello@.

Set up `pranam@` (one a), `hello@` and `info@` as aliases on the same mailbox.
Aliases are free, and people will mistype a less familiar word or default to
hello@ out of habit. A bounced first enquiry is not worth the saving.

## Type

- **Orbitron Bold** — the brand face. Headings, the wordmark, labels. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Inter** — body text. Free on Google Fonts.
- **Arial / Helvetica** — email signatures only, since mail clients do not load custom fonts.

## Please don't

- Put the colour logo on a white background (see above).
- Stretch or squash it — scale proportionally.
- Recolour the wordmark or flatten the gradient.
- Rebuild the lockup by retyping the name — use these files.
- Crowd it. Leave clear space of at least the height of the "V" on every side.

## Minimum sizes

Full lockup: 140px wide on screen, 30mm in print.
Emblem alone: 28px. Below that the drone and eye stop reading.

## Resolution ceiling

The wordmark is live type set in Orbitron, so it is sharp at any size — the
name is never baked into pixels. The emblem is artwork, 805px wide, and that
is the real ceiling: about 68mm at 300dpi.

That covers business cards, the one-pager and every screen use. For anything
larger — a banner, a vehicle wrap, an exhibition stand — ask whoever created
the logo for the original vector file. Scaling the emblem past 68mm will soften.
