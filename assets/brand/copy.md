## 3. Google Business Profile

Free, and it is how people find you in Maps and local search. Set up at **business.google.com**.

**Business name:** `VaayuSights`

**Primary category:** `Engineering consultant`

Google has no category for aerial-data analysis. `Engineering consultant` is the
closest fit for inspection findings and reporting, which is what you sell. If
cleaning turns out to bring in most of your local enquiries, switch the primary
to `Cleaning service` — the primary category drives what you rank for, so follow
where the work actually comes from.

**Additional categories:** `Surveyor` · `Aerial photographer` · `Solar energy equipment supplier` · `Cleaning service`

**Service area:** set the cities or states you will travel to rather than a
street address. This is a service-area business, so you do not need to publish one.

## 6. The question you will get asked

**"Do you fly it yourselves?"**

Answer it straight, because the site already does:

> Capture runs through vetted partner operators we scope and brief to our own
> spec, so the data arrives fit to analyse. The analysis is entirely in-house,
> and our cleaning crews are our own.

That is a stronger answer than claiming a fleet. It says you control the spec
and own the part that matters, and it will not unravel on the second question.
